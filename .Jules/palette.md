## 2024-07-29 - Links Opening in New Tabs

**Learning:** Links that open in a new tab (`target="_blank"`) can be disorienting for users, especially those using screen readers, if they are not warned in advance. Providing a textual clue, even a visually hidden one, is a critical accessibility feature.

**Action:** For all future implementations of external links, I will ensure that a visually hidden span with text like "(opens in new tab)" is included to provide context for assistive technologies. This maintains a clean visual design while significantly improving the user experience for those who rely on screen readers.
